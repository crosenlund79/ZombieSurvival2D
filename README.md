# Red Thaw (working title; repository: ZombieSurvival2D, internal project name Aftergrowth)

A bleak 2D co-op survival and underground bunker game set in a nuclear winter, built in Godot (GDScript).
Civilisation is gone; the people who remain have gone mad. No zombies — the Burnt Ones are human.

> A frozen world. Abandoned places. The remnants of humanity.

**Status:** iteration 015 "The World Reborn" on `iteration/015-world-reborn` — a much larger world (five wilderness regions between the settlements, edge travel on foot and by car, a geographic world map with fog of war), timber cabins and dressed interiors, player-centred visibility, a dirty-ink UI with object-anchored panels, progressive scavenging, locks and a lockpicking game, roaming hordes, dangerous wolves and bears, three hideouts, and three factions (a hostile raider camp, the fenced community of Hearth Gate with trade, repairs, missions and law, and the Lantern Circle) ([report](docs/iterations/015_world_reborn.md)). Before it, iteration 014 "The Five Settlements" on `iteration/014-five-settlements` — side-view building interiors, four new settlements (Corran Junction, Greyhollow with its mine, Stillwater with the four-level Civic Hall, the frozen harbour of Port Halden) on one road network, a Burnt One hideout, firearms (the handgun first), single-player time controls, a 175-item catalogue with icons and tooltips, and contextual loot ([report](docs/iterations/014_five_settlements.md)). Before it, iteration 013 "The Scavenger's Circuit" on `iteration/013-scavengers-circuit` — enterable Ashgrove shops and houses, physical containers searched over time (persistent, never refilling), a Scavenging skill with a Skills screen (K), a developer Progression menu with an XP multiplier, a usable trunk and a wood stove for the shelter ([report](docs/iterations/013_scavengers_circuit.md)). Before it, iteration 012 "Shadows & Depth" on `iteration/012-shadows-and-depth` — Burnt Ones as silhouettes, heat-thawed blood traces that weather erases, and an experimental side-view bunker (F7 compares with the original; [report](docs/iterations/012_shadows_and_depth.md)). Before it, iteration 011 "The World Awakens" — living Burnt Ones (postures, crawling, listening, screeches), cold exposure and dynamic weather, communal sleep, a drivable station wagon, the town of Ashgrove with a cellar base, a regional map, wildlife, a flashlight, the creator's audio inbox and a custom-graphics switch ([report](docs/iterations/011_world_awakens.md)). Built on iteration 010 "Red Thaw" — concept and visual rebirth (burned-shed opening, hunter's cabin, frozen pine woods, abandoned settlement, omens, a frightened survivor, developer-triggered Burnt One hunts) on top of iterations 001–009 (building and defences, crafting, food and equipment, day/night and weather, bunker life). Windows acceptance pending. Play: [`docs/PLAYTEST_GUIDE.md`](docs/PLAYTEST_GUIDE.md).
Godot 4.7.2 · open `project.godot` · press F5. Two players: see the testing guide.
